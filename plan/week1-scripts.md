# Week 1 scripts: season opener (30 s each)

Each Short: clip A (0-15 s) setup, clip B (15-30 s) punchline. Voice: Rico narrates, characters make sounds only.

## 1. Tip-off
A: Rico announces the season opener. Flo bounces on his toes, Teo yawns, Kapi walks in eating a melon slice and sits on the center circle. B: The ref throws the ball up. Everyone sprints, Kapi stays seated, the ball falls into his lap. He rolls it to the hoop, it drops in. Rico: "Zero steps. Zero effort. Two points."

## 2. Flo's big dunk
A: Flo takes off for a huge dunk, slow motion, dramatic music, Rico screaming. B: Flo hangs on the rim by his beak. Kapi walks under, holds out a melon slice. Flo lets go to grab it, falls on the pad. Rico: "The dunk... is technically complete."

## 3. Teo's wall
A: Teo stands in the paint, three opponents try to get around him one by one, each loses time. B: The last attacker gives up and sits down next to him. Teo finally steps one pace forward. Rico: "He moved. Everyone panic."

## 4. Rico loses it
A: Close game, Rico gets louder and louder, mic starts squealing. B: Kapi quietly takes the mic from the booth and says one word: "Chill." The arena goes silent, then everyone laughs and Kapi shoots a buzzer-beater without looking.

## 5. Overtime
A: Tie game, overtime starts, everyone is exhausted, Flo faints dramatically. B: Kapi is the only one still fresh (he never ran). He hits the winning shot from half court, shrugs, and goes back to his melon. Rico: "Season one, game one. Nobody has any idea what just happened."
